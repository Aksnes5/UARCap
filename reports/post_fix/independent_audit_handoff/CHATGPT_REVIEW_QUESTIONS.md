# Questions for independent review

1. Do Oracle3−D3 and Oracle7−Oracle3 genuinely support two bottlenecks
   (selection and integration), or is the second explained by lower-quality
   added evidence?
2. Does the work substantially duplicate the predecessor's k̃ ablation (k̃=7 best
   in-domain)? What minimum additional evidence would make it distinct?
3. Is the six-scale study an independent scientific contribution or merely a
   larger hyperparameter sweep?
4. Is the evidence chain (main grid + in-domain + oracle + cross-dataset)
   sufficient for the claimed conclusions, given 0/72 main-grid tests survive
   FDR?
5. Are non-significant text/visual/in-domain results over-interpreted in the
   reports? Where exactly?
6. Are there data-contamination, statistical or unfair-comparison problems?
   (See AUDIT_01/03/05.)
7. If only one or two additional experiments are possible, which are
   indispensable? Candidates: matched k=0, faithful pseudo-label reranker,
   independent validation split, second generator.
8. Should the authors stop experiments and write now, or run the indispensable
   experiment(s) first?
9. Is the current evidence competitive for an SCI Q3 journal? What would Q2
   require?
10. What is the strongest defensible core-contribution statement for the paper?
