# E3 reserve cohort V2

The original screen retains the fixed lower bound of 150, with at most 157 historically nonoverlapping machine candidates before screening. The reserve rule is frozen independently of new generation. It uses original ACH MCQ data, seed 20261126 and deterministic source deduplication. Exclusions include all 30 Pilot IDs, the full 180-candidate original formal roster, the official VideoUFO TSV, mapped historical MSR-VTT IDs, all debug sources and duplicate or unparseable inputs. The inventory preserves every reason and the source files' SHA-256.

`scripts/e3_reserve_pool.py pool` builds the pool. An authoritative debug-source receipt is required at `data/source_witness/vidhalluc_debug_source_ids.txt`. Source IDs alone establish only exact known overlap. The BN operator supplies a separate real authorization at `deploy/laptop-v2-e3-reserve-approval.json` with `approved`, `approved_by`, `protocol_revision`, `policy`, `pool_sha256` and `max_additional_candidates`. The receipt caps additional screening and binds the exact pool. Media access and any nondefault sampling scope must be approved under that revision.

Continuation starts only if the original count is below 150 and an approved reserve exists. It uses the same Vicuna/Qwen tokenizers, text-screen prompts and threshold, role-blind Qwen verification on the actual 16 frames and dense 64-frame witness, and the same final neutral criteria. Reserve processing is bounded by the authorized prefix. The first eligible reserves in that order fill the deficit. Rejected and surplus qualified rows remain in the audit. If sources, approvals, screens or required distinct candidates are missing, the final gate remains blocked. `E3_INSUFFICIENT_VALID_CANDIDATES` keeps all unrelated work runnable.

The new freeze remains machine-assisted. The generator accepts only matching frame hashes and keeps the legacy Neu in history. Compatible old Video-LLaMA Base/Sup/Con require exact input, prompt, checkpoint and decoding parity. VidHalluc whole-video truth, exact 16-frame visibility and expression receive separate human checks.
