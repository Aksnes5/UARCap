# Evaluator stress tests

The 196 controlled text cases share 14 manually specified logical templates instantiated over 14 entity pairs. The premise makes negative evidence and exclusivity explicit where needed. Thirteen categories test reference NLI and one tests extraction from an explicit conjunction. Correct, paraphrase, alternative action, role reversal, count, color, temporal order, negation, double negation, vague, generic, related object and unmentioned are distinct. The two fact-conjunct rows require identifying both relations. The related-object case tests weak entailment while a mention of an object alone supplies no evidence of the target action.

`scripts/evaluator_stress.py run` invokes Gemma or Llama through the actual offline backend and the production prompt/parsers. The raw ledger resumes by case identifier and config hash. `report` writes per-category accuracy, confusion, parse failure, negation and unmentioned errors, paraphrase stability and overlap of errors. Atom-count and canonical exact alignment are mechanical checks. Non-exact extractions are routed to independent review for omission, merge or valid paraphrase. The closed-form fixture is separate from real generated visual assertions. The latter retain their independently blinded human protocol and probability weights. Only CPU fixture outputs were generated locally.
