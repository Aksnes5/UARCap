# Retrieval Evidence Budgets and Caption Factuality in Video Captioning

## Abstract

External retrieval supplies evidence for video caption generation. This study examines when a larger evidence budget yields a net benefit within fixed generation systems. The historical study, natural retrieval panels, controlled evidence interventions and robustness analyses are reported with separate scopes.
